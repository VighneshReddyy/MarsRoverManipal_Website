01  Who we are

About Us

Mars Rover Manipal (MRM) is a multidisciplinary student engineering team from Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), dedicated to designing and developing next-generation robotic rovers for extraterrestrial exploration. The team focuses on advancing robotics, autonomous systems, and innovative engineering solutions for planetary missions.

We primarily participate in the European Rover Challenge (ERC), an international robotics competition organized by the European Space Foundation and hosted in Kraków, Poland. In ERC 2026, Mars Rover Manipal secured 10th place worldwide, competing against university teams from across the globe.

Beyond competitions, MRM provides students with hands-on engineering experience through interdisciplinary work in robotics, artificial intelligence, autonomous navigation, mechanical design, electronics, and software development.
